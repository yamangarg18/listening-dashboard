**Executive Summary**
=====================

Physical AI --- AI systems that interact with and operate in the
physical world --- is penetrating virtually every sector of the global
economy. This taxonomy identifies 33 primary industries and 400+
sub-industries where Physical AI has current, emerging, or speculative
applications across all modalities: humanoid robots, autonomous mobile
robots (AMRs), drones/UAVs, robotic arms, autonomous vehicles, surgical
robots, exoskeletons, quadrupeds, underwater robots, and more.

**Market Scale:** Surgical robotics (\$9-13B), warehouse automation
(\$9.3B), autonomous vehicles (\$274B in 2025). Goldman Sachs projects
humanoid robots alone reaching \$38B by 2035; Morgan Stanley sees a \$5T
ecosystem by 2050.

**Key Patterns:** Inspection is the universal entry point across all
industries. Logistics serves as the beachhead for humanoids. Deformable
object manipulation (textiles, food, laundry) is the key technical
barrier. The market is bifurcating into a near-term \$50B wave
(2025-2028) of structured automation and a long-term wave (2030-2050) of
general-purpose humanoid capability.

**Scope:** This document covers deployed/commercial systems, early-stage
R&D (3-5 year horizon), and speculative/long-horizon applications. All
Physical AI modalities are included.

**1. Manufacturing**
====================

*The largest existing market for Physical AI, with 4.7 million
industrial robots in active service globally and 542,000 new units
installed in 2024. Automotive and electronics account for nearly half of
all deployments.*

**Automotive Manufacturing**
----------------------------

Robotic arms (welding, painting, assembly), cobots, AGVs/AMRs, humanoids
(Tesla Optimus, Figure 02 at BMW). Tasks: spot/arc/laser welding, body
sanding, windshield installation, engine assembly, quality inspection.
\~30% of all industrial robots serve this sector.

**Electronics and PCB Assembly**
--------------------------------

SCARA robots, delta robots, SMT placement, vision-guided arms, cobots.
Foxconn undergoing massive robotics transformation with NVIDIA.
Pick-and-place of micro-components, soldering, AOI testing, conformal
coating, wafer handling.

**Semiconductor Fabrication**
-----------------------------

Cleanroom SCARA/6-axis robots, wafer-handling systems, AGVs, inspection
systems. Wafer transport, lithography support, die bonding, wire
bonding, defect detection via AI vision.

**Food and Beverage Processing**
--------------------------------

Delta robots (high-speed), SCARA, 6-axis arms, cobots, AMRs, quadrupeds.
Sorting, palletizing, packaging, quality inspection (contamination
detection), portioning, meat/poultry processing.

**Pharmaceutical Manufacturing**
--------------------------------

Cleanroom-rated arms, cobots, AGVs, SCARA robots. Drug compounding,
vial/syringe filling, packaging, labeling, quality inspection. Market
projected to reach \$2.1B by 2028.

**Aerospace Manufacturing**
---------------------------

cobots, inspection drones. Drilling, riveting, composite layup,
fuselage/wing assembly, painting, NDT, sealant application.

**Battery Manufacturing**
-------------------------

Cleanroom 6-axis arms, AGVs, cobots. Electrode stacking/winding, cell

**Metal Fabrication**
---------------------

Welding arms, laser cutting robots, press brake tending robots, cobots.

grinding/deburring.

**Plastics and Injection Molding**
----------------------------------

Cartesian/gantry robots, 6-axis arms, cobots. Part removal from molds,

**Heavy Machinery and Shipbuilding**

**Consumer Goods and FMCG**

---------------------------------------

Drones and quadrupeds performing visual, thermal, and ultrasonic
inspection appear in virtually every industry - from nuclear plants to
coral reefs. This is the lowest-risk, highest-ROI starting point for any
Physical AI ecosystem.

**Logistics as Humanoid Beachhead**
-----------------------------------

Agility Digit, Figure AI, and Tesla Optimus are all targeting warehouse

other sectors.

**Deformable Object Manipulation as Key Barrier**
-------------------------------------------------

Sewing, laundry folding, food handling, and textile processing all
bottleneck on the same fundamental challenge of handling soft, flexible
materials.

**Near-Term Wave (2025-2028)**

Dominated by industrial arms, AMRs, quadrupeds, and drones performing
structured tasks in controlled environments. Worth roughly \$50B today,
growing at 15-25% annually.

**Long-Term Wave (2030-2050)**

General-purpose humanoids performing unstructured tasks across every
